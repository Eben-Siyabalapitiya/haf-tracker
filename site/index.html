    About the numbers

    Totals are the weighted votes shown on the public ballot. Newsletter-confirmed votes can count double, so they are not a count of unique voters. Every change here is the difference between two observed totals, not proof of how many people voted.

    How it updates

    A GitHub Actions job checks the vote totals every 30 seconds and stores every change. This page checks for new data every 20 seconds, so new votes usually appear within 1–2 minutes. It is frequently updated, not second-by-second live.
